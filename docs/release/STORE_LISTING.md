## Full description (EN)

Shishur Dinlipi (শিশুর দিনলিপি) helps parents remember and protect a child’s story — privately, on your device.

Capture journals, funny moments, achievements, growth, milestones, school events, vaccinations, illnesses, medicines, and doctor visits. Browse a calm timeline, photos, albums, and generate an offline “Year in Review” PDF.

Your data stays on your phone. Optional PIN and biometrics lock the app. Create encrypted local backups you can restore anytime — no account required.
